# content calendar

A small, calm planner for short-form content: posts, ideas, to-dos, brand collabs and the dreams you're working toward. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step, no account, no tracking.

## Use it

- **Online:** open the site and, on your phone, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** (iPhone, in Safari) or **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (Android). It opens full screen, like an app, and works offline.
- **On your computer:** download the files and double-click `index.html`.

Everything you add is saved **in your own browser, on your own device**. Nothing is sent anywhere. Each device keeps its own copy, so use **⋯ → export backup** and **import backup** to move your plans between devices or keep a safe copy.

## What's inside

- **home**: a hype line, this week's progress, today's to-dos and the dreams you're working toward.
- **to-dos**: list, week and month views, with smart adding ("film the hook tomorrow !", "#work fri"), ⭐ important, repeat and notes.
- **board**: 💡 idea → ✍️ scripted → 🎬 filmed → ✅ posted.
- **calendar**: day, week and month views. Drag posts between days (and across weeks or months), and drag unscheduled ideas in from the ideas drawer.
- **ideas**: your idea bank, a 6-idea generator, and 🎲 pick for me.
- **collabs**: a brand pipeline (cards or an editable **sheet** you can sort, search and export as CSV) with follow-up reminders, payment tracking, message templates, a rate card and a pitching playbook.
- **lines**: your signature lines, insertable straight into scripts.

## Make it yours

Add a `personal.js` next to `index.html` that sets `window.CC_PERSONAL` (your series, starter ideas, dreams, signature lines, hype lines, profile and message templates) and the app uses those as its starter content. It's listed in `.gitignore`, so your personal version never gets uploaded.
